# Welcome to Cal.com Booking Assistant! 👋

This is a chat interface for managing your Cal.com bookings. You can:

1. List your bookings
2. Create new bookings
3. Cancel existing bookings

## Examples

Try these commands:

- "Show me my bookings"
- "Book a meeting for tomorrow at 3pm"
- "Cancel my booking with ID xyz123"

## Settings

Use the "Change Email" button to update your email address for bookings.
